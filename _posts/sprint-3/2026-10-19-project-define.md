## Project and Problem

Our project is the **SFI Foundation website**, which helps people find motorsports safety standards and equipment information.

The existing prototype already has specification search. My proposed improvement is to make the results easier to narrow down and understand, especially for someone who knows the equipment name but not its SFI specification number.

## Who It Helps

Racers and equipment buyers need to find relevant specifications and open the official documents without guessing which result applies to their equipment.

## My Planned Contribution

I plan to improve the search and category filters, show why a result matches, and give a clear message when nothing is found. I would work on the frontend search experience and the backend logic that returns matching records. I still need to agree on this feature's ownership with my team.

## User Stories

- As a racer, I want to search by equipment name or spec number so I can find the right records.
- As a buyer, I want category filters to narrow my search so unrelated results do not appear.
- As a new user, I want clear result labels and official document links so I can check the information myself.
- If nothing matches, I want a clear message and a way to reset my search.

## What Success Looks Like

Search and category filters work together, results show the product and spec number, and available PDF links open the source documents. No matches and connection errors have different messages. The feature helps locate standards; it does not certify equipment.
